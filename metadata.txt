about=
    02 Geo3D Analyzer turns a DEM and vector layers into an interactive 3D scene in three steps (terrain, objects, open in 3D) and runs GPU-accelerated spatial and environmental analyses, returning styled results directly to QGIS as GeoTIFF and GeoPackage layers.
    
    KEY CAPABILITIES:
    - Three-Step Workflow: Ground elevation mesh (DEM), extruded objects (buildings, walls, trees, poles) from fields or expressions with QGIS symbology, and single-click 3D launch.
    - Solar and Daylight Analyses: Sun hours, shadow casting, Sky View Factor (SVF), facade daylight (Vertical Sky Component under CIE overcast sky), annual solar irradiation, and rooftop and facade PV yield.
    - Microclimate and Thermal Comfort: Surface energy balance with Bowen ratio partitioning, ground surface temperature, tree canopy evapotranspiration cooling, Physiological Equivalent Temperature (PET) and UTCI heat stress, 24-hour diurnal thermal studies, and interactive 3D tree planting.
    - Ventilation, Air Quality and Acoustics: Street canyon ventilation efficiency (VEI), normalized air age, traffic pollutant dispersion (NO2 and PM2.5) with tree filtration, and 3D acoustic propagation (ISO 9613-2 proxy) with quiet-facade detection.
    - Green View and Solar Envelope: Panoramic pedestrian Green View Index (GVI) and Knowles and BRE 3D Solar Envelope height limits and floor capacity.
    - Urban Comfort and Environment: Pedestrian wind screening with animated streamlines, heat-island morphology, approximate Local Climate Zones (LCZ), and seasonal outdoor comfort around buildings using EnergyPlus (EPW/TMYx) weather files.
    - Topographic and Hydrologic Analyses: Slope, aspect, hillshade, curvature, Topographic Position Index (TPI), ruggedness (TRI), topographic wetness index (TWI), dynamic cross-sections, flood inundation, and cut and fill earthwork volume calculations.
    - Planning Regulations and Optimization: BRE 209 daylight and sunlight compliance checks with shareable threshold profiles, side-by-side scenario comparison (A vs B), and multi-objective NSGA-II building height optimization.
    - Interoperability and Export: Synchronized multi-panel view (up to 8 synced panels), 8K high-resolution capture, PDF documentation sheets, standalone offline HTML packages, GLB 3D models, and animated MP4 video recording.
    
    Runs entirely on your local machine via an embedded web server (127.0.0.1); no cloud dependencies or data uploads. Compatible with QGIS 3.28 through QGIS 4.x.
