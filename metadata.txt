about=02 Geo3D Analyzer turns a DEM and your vector layers into a 3D scene in the browser in three steps (terrain, objects, open in 3D) and runs the analyses planners ask of a 3D model. Buildings, walls, trees and poles are extruded from any height field or expression, stand on sloping ground and are coloured by their QGIS style or any attribute; OpenStreetMap buildings (with building parts) and trees can be downloaded for the scene. GPU analyses cover sun hours, shade and shadow studies, sky view factor, daylight on facades as the vertical sky component under the CIE overcast sky, annual irradiation with rooftop and facade PV yield, viewsheds, pedestrian wind screening, UTCI heat stress, heat-island morphology and approximate Local Climate Zones, and a year of outdoor comfort around every building by season from an EnergyPlus weather file (the nearest TMYx station is one click away). Terrain analyses add slope, aspect, curvature, topographic position, ruggedness and a wetness index, sections, flooding and cut and fill. A planning check marks each building pass or fail against BRE 209 screening criteria with shareable threshold profiles; scenarios A and B are compared per building; an NSGA-II search optimises building heights within those constraints. Results open side by side in up to eight synced panels, export as 8K images, PDF sheets, offline HTML, GLB and video, and return to QGIS as GeoTIFFs and building fields, styled in the 2D map and in the QGIS 3D view. Developed with feedback from educational workflows at Dokuz Eylul University, Department of City and Regional Planning.
