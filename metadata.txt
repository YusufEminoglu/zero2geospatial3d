about=02 Geo3D Analyzer turns a DEM and your vector layers into a 3D scene in the browser in three steps (terrain, objects, open in 3D) and runs the analyses planners ask of a 3D model. Buildings, walls, trees and poles are extruded from any height field or expression and coloured by the QGIS style or any attribute. GPU analyses cover sun hours and shadow share for a day, sky view factor, annual solar irradiation and rooftop and facade PV yield (with an EnergyPlus weather file or a clear-sky estimate), viewsheds, pedestrian wind screening, UTCI heat stress with a SolarCal mean radiant temperature, and heat-island morphology indicators (plan and frontal area fractions, canyon ratio, roughness, approximate Local Climate Zones). Terrain tools draw sections with building silhouettes, flood a water level and compute cut and fill. Scenario A and B layers are compared as difference maps, and an NSGA-II search trades floor area against sun on open ground. Every result can go back to QGIS as a GeoTIFF and as fields on a copy of the building layer. Climate rasters are draped on the ground and averaged per building, and the nearest TMYx weather file can be downloaded from climate.onebuilding.org. Developed with feedback from educational workflows at Dokuz Eylul University, Department of City and Regional Planning.
